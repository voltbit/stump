You can use Stump to host your own book club! This guide will overview how this feature works, what functionalities are available, and walk you through the process of setting up a book club in Stump.

## Basic requirements

To set up a book club in Stump, you'll need:

- A Stump instance
- A managed user account (otherwise, it would be a club of one)

You don't even technically need any books on the server to start a book club, either, since a club's reading list, schedules, and suggestions can all reference 'external' books (a title/author/URL) instead of a book stored in one of your libraries.

## How it works

Book clubs in Stump are effectively just a group of users that all have access to special pages and functionalities that are only available to members of a given book club. There can be multiple book clubs in a single Stump instance, and users can be members of multiple book clubs.

Creating a club makes you its **Creator** and its first member. Beyond that, membership is by invitation or by a club Admin/Creator adding you directly — see the [Roles and Permissions](/docs/guides/features/book-clubs/rbac) guide for how clubs are discovered, how invitations work, and what each role can do.

Once you're a member of a club, a few more tabs become available on the club's page:

- **Suggestions** — propose and vote on what the club should read next, see the [Books, Reading List & Suggestions](/docs/guides/features/book-clubs/books) guide
- **Members** — the club's roster
- **Settings** — general club info, member management, role labels, the reading list, and scheduling. Any member can open Settings, but most of what's in it (editing club info, managing members and invitations, the reading list, and schedules) is Admin/Creator-only — see [Roles and Permissions](/docs/guides/features/book-clubs/rbac)

## Web vs. mobile

Book clubs work on both the web app and the mobile app, but they don't (yet) have identical surfaces:

- **Discovering, suggesting, and organizing** — the Explore page, the Members roster, the Suggestions tab, and full reading list/scheduling management (in Settings) are currently **web only**.
- **Discussions/chat** are currently **mobile only** — see [Social Features](/docs/guides/features/book-clubs/social-features).
- Clubs list, creating a club, invitations (viewing/accepting/declining), the club home page (including adding and completing the current book, and a preview of a few members), and basic club settings (info, leaving, deleting) work on **both**.
